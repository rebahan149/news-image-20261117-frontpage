# The Morning Atlas — Tuesday 17 November 2026

Entry for the make.fun bounty [news-image-20261117](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261117): sum up 17 November 2026's world news in one image.

A single front page that sums up one full day of world news — Tuesday 17 November 2026: **44 stories, six continents, one page**, with **13 illustrated panels**.

## What the page shows

- **13 illustrated panels**, each with an original flat-vector drawing: Ukraine's drones bring down a stretch of the bridge to Crimea; Iranian drones hunt US radar in Bahrain as tankers burn in Hormuz; Trump uses the Quad summit in Manila to unveil minerals pacts; Netanyahu's opponents take the Knesset chair; Mongolia's President Khurelsukh says he will step down; AI-stock jitters sink Wall Street; Nvidia's board says no to Huang's return as revenue doubles to $115.8bn; the US and Mexico lose measles-free status; rows over the draft deal at COP31 in Antalya; a military plane crash kills 19 in Siberia; over 100,000 march for better pay in France; Xi and Carney agree more tariff cuts; the UN counts 2,230 civilians killed in Sudan this year.
- **A "Where it happened" world map** with pins 1–13 matched to the panel stories.
- **31 more stories** (briefs 14–44): Mondlane's trial, Sözcü TV seized, Ukraine peace talks, the strained US fleet, the NSA network outage, the $50bn air-traffic fix, Waymo's freeway halt, California v OpenAI, killer-robot rules debated by 128 states, UK sanctions on settlement firms, Guinea's 1,263 gold-miner arrests, Ecuador's fishermen suing the US, 1,441 drones intercepted over Moscow, Japan's jets scrambled near Fukuoka, Serbia's student list, Germany's Gaza aid call, Jordan blocking Telegram, Iraq's oil exports via Turkey — and more.

## How it was made

The page is a single self-contained `index.html` (offline fonts, inline SVG illustrations drawn for this entry) rendered to a 1024×1024 PNG. It was designed and built in-session with Muse, modelling the leaders' recipe — many illustrated panels, a map, dense briefs — with an original masthead, layout and artwork. `main.py` emits the judged image and its dossier.
